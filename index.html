Consider asking
  • Location & description of pain/symptoms?
  • Mechanism / onset of injury (acute vs. insidious)?
  • Date of injury & date of last competition/practice?
  • Prior history of this injury? Prior surgeries/imaging?

  • Pain pattern — constant vs. intermittent, time of day?
  • Functional limitations (sport-specific, ADLs)?
  • Prior treatment received & response to treatment?
  • Relevant past medical / surgical / family history?
  • Current medications / supplements?
